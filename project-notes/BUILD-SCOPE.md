# Levett Carpentry POC - Scope and decision log

## Decisions confirmed

- Personal project, not PMY. GitHub account `danielproduct`.
- No final branding or logo at this gate.
- Residential carpentry for homes, Bishop's Stortford and London. Includes indoor and outdoor work; listed categories are not exhaustive.
- Short homepage: Show Us Your Space, Before & After, Clever Spaces, Meet the Maker, footer.
- More detail on individual pages; contextual enquiry button on those pages.
- Free static GitHub Pages demo, one new public repository.
- No real enquiries, payments, client data or user login.
- Daniel maintains the website; code and image assets are intentionally simple.

## What the POC proves

1. Can a person understand the main CTA on a phone?
2. Can they discover an example and move straight into an enquiry?
3. Can they choose the correct project type and select up to three photos?
4. Can Daniel maintain examples by editing a single JS data file?

## What is still outstanding before a real launch

- Confirm Levett versus Levent spelling against the carpenter's business materials.
- Client sign-off and approved project photography (before/after pairs, permission).
- Approved business description and maker biography.
- Business contact email, live form workflow, data retention and privacy notice.
- Domain and commercial hosting choice.
- Production form notifications and Google Sheets/Drive setup.

## Later-phase ideas (not in POC)

- Automated portfolio and image upload workflow
